Activities required across all three dimensions

  • Months 1–3: Begin formal feasibility study (PRL); identify multilingual-learner community partners and establish paid advisory cadence (PeoML); translate equity goals into design-decision criteria with documented trail (ProML).
  • Months 4–7: Build integrated prototype with stable infrastructure (PRL); bring at least one design decision through community advisory review (PeoML); design simulated-classroom test as subgroup-first study with pre-registered analysis plan (ProML).
  • Months 8–10: Test prototype in lightly-simulated classroom environment (PRL); recruit at least one community member into paid team or advisory role (PeoML); run prototype test with subgroup-aware data collection (ProML).
  • Months 11–12: Produce evidence package — prototype results, scaling requirements, documented consultation mechanism, pre-registered subgroup analysis plan. This is the artifact that unlocks the next funding cycle.

The point: a credible R&D plan develops all three dimensions in parallel. WriteAhead's prototype work alone (PRL 3 → 5) would produce a working tool with no equity discipline behind it. The three readiness scores moving together are what make the evidence credible to a funder, useful to a district, and accountable to the multilingual learners it claims to serve.
