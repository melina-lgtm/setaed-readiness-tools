Why three dimensions

Three independent, structured ladders do work that narrative review — and any one-dimensional score — can't.

Shared language → payable milestones

Funders and ventures score projects on the same ladder. Grant tranches can be released against named transitions (PRL 5 → 6, ProML 3 → 4) instead of vague deliverables.

Shared expectations with funders

Each stage specifies what reasonable progress looks like, surfacing authentic progress even when prototype work stalls and only People or Process readiness advances.

Apples-to-apples across the portfolio

Same rubric across all grantees: comparable scoring, distributions, outliers, longitudinal trend lines. Collapses inter-reviewer drift.

Catches what 1D frameworks miss

A prototype can hit PRL 9 (scaled across thousands of classrooms) and still be inequitable. The People and Process ladders surface theory-of-change gaps and equity exposure that PRL alone cannot.
