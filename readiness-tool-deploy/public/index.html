PRISM is SETA-ED's portfolio management platform for evaluating education R&D ventures across three readiness dimensions: Prototype, People, and Process. Defined by SETA-ED and adapted from the R&D portfolio practices of DARPA, ARPA-E, IARPA, ARPA-H, and other Advanced Research Project Agencies. Two purpose-built interfaces share the same rubric — collapsing information asymmetry between funders and ventures.

The three readiness dimensions

Every project has three readiness scores at any moment, one per dimension:

Prototype Readiness Levels (PRL) · 9 levels People Readiness Levels (PeoML) · 5 levels Process Readiness Levels (ProML) · 5 levels

A prototype can reach PRL 9 — sustained, scaled use across thousands of classrooms — and still be inequitable in ways no amount of additional prototype work can fix. The People and Process ladders catch what PRL alone cannot.
