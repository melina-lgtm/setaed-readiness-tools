Portfolio Readiness Insight & Scoring Methodology

See your portfolio's readiness in three dimensions.

PRISM is SETA-ED's portfolio management platform for benchmarking and tracking education R&D ventures across three independent readiness dimensions — Prototype, People, and Process. A structured, three-dimensional rubric gives funders and ventures shared language, turns progress into observable milestones, surfaces honest progress even when prototype work stalls, and catches what one-dimensional frameworks miss. Defined by SETA-ED and adapted from DARPA / ARPA-E / IARPA / ARPA-H portfolio practice. Two purpose-built interfaces share the same rubric.

Why three dimensions

Every PRISM project has three readiness scores at any moment, one per dimension:

Prototype Readiness Levels (PRL) · 9 levels People Readiness Levels (PeoML) · 5 levels Process Readiness Levels (ProML) · 5 levels

Three independent, structured ladders do work narrative review can't:

Shared language → payable milestones

Funders and ventures score on the same ladder. Grant tranches release against named transitions (PRL 5 → 6, ProML 3 → 4) instead of vague deliverables.

Shared expectations with funders

Each stage specifies what reasonable progress looks like — surfacing honest progress even when prototype work stalls and only People or Process advances.

Apples-to-apples across the portfolio

Same rubric across all grantees: comparable scoring, distributions, outliers, longitudinal trend lines. Collapses inter-reviewer drift.

Catches what 1D frameworks miss

A prototype can hit PRL 9 — scaled across thousands of classrooms — and still be inequitable. The People and Process ladders surface theory-of-change gaps and equity exposure that PRL alone cannot.
