The three readiness frameworks

Each tool evaluates an R&D project on three independent ladders that together describe what's actually true about a venture's readiness:

Prototype Readiness Levels (PRL) · 9 levels People Readiness Levels (PeoML) · 5 levels Process Readiness Levels (ProML) · 5 levels

A prototype can reach PRL 9 — sustained, scaled use across thousands of classrooms — and still be inequitable in ways that no amount of additional prototype work can fix. The People and Process ladders catch what PRL alone cannot.
